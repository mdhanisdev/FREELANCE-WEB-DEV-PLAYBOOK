# GET CLIENTS [ FIND THE WORK ]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## THE GOAL OF THIS STEP

No client, no project. This step is about filling your pipeline with
real, qualified leads who have money and a genuine need, and filtering
out the tire-kickers before they waste your week.

There are two roads to a client: platforms and direct. Use both.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## WHERE CLIENTS COME FROM

```text
PLATFORMS (escrow + safety, lower margin)
  Upwork ..... biggest volume, competitive, escrow protects payment
  Fiverr ..... productized gigs, buyers come to you, race to bottom
  Contra ..... freelancer-friendly, no commission on many plans
  Toptal ..... vetted/elite, hard to enter, strong rates

  + Payment protection, dispute handling, built-in traffic
  - Platform takes a cut, more price pressure, they own the client

DIRECT (higher margin, you own the relationship)
  LinkedIn ......... post, connect, DM decision-makers
  Cold outreach .... email/DM local + niche businesses with a real hook
  Referrals ........ ask every happy client "who else do you know?"
  Portfolio site ... your own domain, ranks you, closes for you
  Local business ... restaurants, clinics, trades that need a site
  Communities ...... Discord, Slack groups, indie/founder forums

  + Full margin, direct trust, repeat work
  - You do the finding, no escrow, you handle the risk yourself
```

Start on platforms for cash flow and reviews. Build direct channels in
parallel so you are not renting your business from Upwork forever.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## BUILD A PORTFOLIO + PICK A NICHE

Clients hire proof, not promises. Before you chase leads, have something
to point at.

```text
□ Ship 2-3 solid projects (real or self-initiated) you can show
□ Build your own portfolio site (Next.js/TS - eat your own cooking)
□ Write case studies: problem -> what you built -> result
□ Pick a niche: "Next.js web apps for X" beats "I do everything"
□ A niche lets you charge more, market faster, and get referrals
```

A generalist competes with the whole world. A specialist competes with
almost no one.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## QUALIFY THE LEAD

Not every inquiry is a client. Before you invest time, confirm four
things. If any are missing, slow down.

```text
✓ BUDGET ......... do they have money, and is it in your range?
✓ TIMELINE ....... is the deadline real and sane, not "yesterday"?
✓ DECISION-MAKER . are you talking to the person who says yes and pays?
✓ REAL PROJECT ... is this defined and funded, or a daydream?
```

You do not need exact numbers on the first message, but you need signals
that all four exist. A lead missing all four is a hobby, not a job.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## THE FIRST REPLY

Your first response sets the tone. Be fast, professional, and steer
toward a call. Do not write a quote from a one-line message.

```text
□ Reply within a few hours if you can
□ Thank them, show you read their message (reference a detail)
□ Ask 2-3 sharp questions (goal, timeline, rough budget)
□ Propose a short call to scope it properly
□ Send your Calendly link so they can book without back-and-forth
```

Example tone: "Thanks for reaching out. That sounds like a good fit for
what I do. So I can give you real numbers, could we do a quick 20-minute
call this week? Here is my calendar: <link>." Keep it human, keep it
short.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## RED FLAGS (WALK AWAY)

```text
✗ "No real budget yet" / dodges every money question
✗ "It should be simple, just a quick thing" (it never is)
✗ Equity-only or "exposure" instead of payment
✗ Vague scope that changes every message
✗ Wants free work / spec sample to "see if you are good"
✗ Rude, pushy, or disrespectful before you have even started
```

One bad client costs more than three good ones earn. Saying no is a
business skill.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## TOOLS FOR THIS STEP

```text
LinkedIn ................. direct outreach, posting, credibility
Upwork / Fiverr / Contra . platform leads with escrow protection
Portfolio site ........... your proof, your closer (own domain)
Calendly ................. let leads book a call without email tag
Lead tracker ............. Notion or Trello board of every lead + stage
```

Track every lead in one board: Name / Source / Budget / Stage / Next
action. What you do not track, you forget, and forgotten leads are lost
money.
