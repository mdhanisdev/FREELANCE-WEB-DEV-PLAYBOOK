## WHAT THIS IS

This folder is not a pile of notes. It is a GUIDE you follow in order.

Start at folder 01 and walk forward to folder 13. If you follow each
numbered step, you go from zero (no client) to a delivered, fully-paid
website with your hands clean and nothing hanging over you.

Each numbered folder is one step of a real freelance project. Do them
in order. Do not skip. When the current job ends, loop back to 01 and
start the next one.

> **Running a project?** Copy `00 - PROJECT CHECKLIST (PRINT ME).md` — the
> entire pathway as tickboxes on one page. Tick as you go; open the matching
> numbered folder whenever you need the detail.

## ABOUT 07 - TECH STACK

Step 07 is different from the others. It is not a single guide, it is a
TECHNOLOGY LIBRARY you pull from for every build.

It is split into core categories (framework, database, auth, hosting,
payments, and so on) plus an ADDONS section for extras (email, analytics,
CMS, search, and more). Every category and every addon has a
`( Default )` file: the go-to choice you reach for when the client has
no strong opinion. Read the default, use it unless there is a reason not
to.

## THE 3 GOLDEN RULES

These three rules run through every step. Break them and you lose money,
control, or sleep.

```text
✓ MONEY
  Never work for free and never hand over everything before the money
  lands. Deposit up front -> milestone payments during -> final payment
  clears BEFORE full handoff. No exceptions.

✓ OWNERSHIP
  The client owns their business assets, not you. Domain, hosting,
  database, Stripe account, and all API keys live in THEIR accounts.
  You get access to build. You do not hold their business hostage and
  you are not the single point of failure.

✓ LEGAL
  Intellectual property transfers to the client ONLY on full payment.
  A signed contract says so. After handoff your hands are clean: no
  lingering access, no secrets on your machine, no open liability.
```
