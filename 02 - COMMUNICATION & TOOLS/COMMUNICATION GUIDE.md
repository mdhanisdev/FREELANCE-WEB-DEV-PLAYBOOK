# COMMUNICATION & TOOLS [ STAY IN CONTROL ]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## THE GOAL OF THIS STEP

Most freelance projects do not fail on code. They fail on communication:
lost decisions, silent weeks, scope that crept in through a chat message
nobody wrote down. This step sets up your channels and your rules so the
project stays clear, documented, and calm.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## CHANNELS AND WHEN TO USE EACH

Pick the right tool for the message. Using everything for everything is
how things get lost.

```text
EMAIL .................... formal, decisions, records
  Proposals, contracts, invoices, sign-offs, anything you may need
  to prove later. If it matters legally or financially, it goes here.

SLACK / WHATSAPP BUSINESS  quick chat, day-to-day
  Fast questions, small updates, "sending preview now". Keep it light.
  Decisions made here get copied to email or the board.

GOOGLE MEET / ZOOM ....... live calls
  Kickoff, milestone reviews, anything where tone matters or you are
  going in circles in text. Always follow a call with a written recap.

LOOM ..................... async demos and walkthroughs
  Record your screen: show progress, explain a build, walk a bug.
  Saves meetings, respects time zones, gives the client something to
  replay. Great for "here is what I built this week".
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## THE RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

```text
✓ ONE CHANNEL FOR DECISIONS
  Pick one place (usually email or the shared board) where decisions
  are official. Chat is chat; it does not count until it is written
  in the decision channel.

✓ EVERYTHING IMPORTANT IN WRITING
  Verbal agreement on a call? Fine. Then you send the written recap.
  "As discussed, we agreed X. Reply to confirm." No paper, no promise.

✓ WEEKLY UPDATE, ALWAYS
  Send a short update every week even if it is "still working on the
  dashboard, on track for Friday." Silence makes clients anxious and
  anxious clients micromanage.

✓ SET RESPONSE-TIME EXPECTATIONS
  Tell them your hours and turnaround up front ("I reply within one
  business day, I do not work weekends"). Then you are never rude for
  not answering at 11pm.

✓ SHARE PREVIEW LINKS
  Give them a live preview URL so progress is visible without a call.
  Seeing it real kills a hundred "how is it going?" messages.

✓ SCOPE CHANGES GO IN WRITING -> PAID CHANGE REQUESTS
  Any new ask that is not in the agreed scope gets written down and
  priced as a change request. "Happy to add that - it is outside the
  current scope, here is the cost and timeline." Never absorb creep.
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## THE SCOPE-CHANGE FLOW

```text
  Client asks for something new
         |
         v
  Is it in the signed scope?  --- yes ---> just build it
         |
         no
         v
  Write it down: what, why, effort
         |
         v
  Quote it: price + new timeline impact
         |
         v
  Client approves in writing  --- no ---> it does not get built
         |
         yes
         v
  Add to scope, invoice, then build
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## THE KICKOFF CALL

Your first real call sets the whole tone. Run it, do not just attend it.

```text
□ Introductions + confirm who the decision-maker is
□ Restate the goal in your words, get them to confirm it
□ Walk the high-level scope and what is NOT included
□ Agree communication: which channel for what, your hours
□ Agree cadence: weekly update day, preview links, review points
□ Confirm money structure (deposit -> milestones -> final)
□ Note next steps and owners
□ AFTER the call: send a written recap and ask them to confirm
```

A clean kickoff prevents 90% of the arguments that would otherwise show
up in week four.

## TOOLS FOR THIS STEP

```text
Email .................... formal record, decisions, documents
Slack .................... team-style project chat
WhatsApp Business ........ quick client chat, mobile-friendly
Google Meet / Zoom ....... live calls and reviews
Loom ..................... async screen walkthroughs and demos
Calendly ................. book calls without the back-and-forth
Notion / ClickUp board ... shared tasks, decisions, status
Figma comments ........... design feedback pinned to the actual UI
```

Keep the stack small. One decision channel, one chat channel, one board,
one call tool. More tools means more places for things to hide.
